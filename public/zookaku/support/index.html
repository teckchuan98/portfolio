Playing

How do I play?

Drag across the squares to fence a home around each marker. A home must cover exactly as many squares as the number on its marker, every home holds exactly one marker, and no square may be left empty. Tap a fence to take it down. You can see the rules again at any time from Settings > How to play.

What do the ? markers and the mist mean?

A ? marker can be any size; its number is revealed when the park is finished. Mist hides the numbers of the markers under it, and a correct home clears the mist next to it.

How do I get more hints?

You start with 3 free hints. You earn 1 hint for each park you solve Flawless (no mistakes and no hints), and 1 free hint each day you come back. You can also watch a short video for 1 hint.

Your progress

Will I lose my progress if I get a new iPhone or reinstall the game?

No, as long as iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account. ZooKaKu backs up your progress to your own iCloud account and restores it automatically when you open the game on a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, while signed in to the same Apple Account.

My progress did not come back. What can I do?

  • Check that you are signed in to the same Apple Account as before.
  • Check that iCloud is turned on in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.
  • Open ZooKaKu while connected to the internet and give it a minute to sync.

If it still does not appear, email us and we will help.

Ads and privacy

Why does ZooKaKu show ads?

Short ads keep ZooKaKu free to play. Ads never appear in the middle of a park, and none appear during your first parks.

How do I change whether ads are personalised?

ZooKaKu asks once, using Apple's tracking prompt, whether it may show ads that suit you better. You can change your answer at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you can also review your choices with the Privacy choices button in ZooKaKu's Settings.
