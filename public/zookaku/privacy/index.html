1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account, and we do not operate servers that receive information about you.
  • We do not collect or store personal information ourselves.
  • Game progress is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access.
  • The App shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. Ads are personalised only if you allow it when the App asks for permission to track. Otherwise, ads are non-personalised.
  • You can change your tracking choice at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

The App does not ask you to create an account or to provide your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information.

2.2 Information stored on your device

The App stores your game data locally on your device, including the levels you have completed, the animals you have collected, the number of hints you hold and your preferences (sound, music and vibration). This data is used only to operate the App and is not transmitted to us.

2.3 iCloud backup

If iCloud is enabled for your Apple Account, a copy of your game data is stored in your iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so that your progress can be restored on a new device or after reinstalling the App. This copy is stored and managed by Apple under your Apple Account. We have no access to it.

2.4 Information collected by third parties

Our advertising partner collects certain information automatically when the App shows advertisements, as described in Section 3. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

3.1 Advertising and tracking

After you have played a few levels, the App uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt to ask whether it may track you.

  • If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), together with your activity in the App and in other apps and websites, to show you ads that match your interests and to measure how those ads perform.
  • If you do not allow tracking, or have turned tracking off on your device, the App cannot access your advertising identifier and requests non-personalised ads only. Non-personalised ads are chosen using contextual information, such as the App itself and your approximate location, rather than a profile of your interests.

Your choice does not change how the App works. You can change it at any time in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. All advertising in the App is limited to content suitable for general audiences.

3.2 Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App shows Google's consent message when it first opens. The message asks for your consent before advertising technologies store or access information on your device, or use it to personalise ads. You can review or change your choices at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in the App's Settings.

4. How information is used

Information processed in connection with the App is used only to:

  • save and restore your game progress and preferences;
  • display and measure advertisements and prevent advertising fraud (by Google, as described above); and
  • personalise advertisements, only if you have allowed tracking and, where required, given consent.

We do not ourselves build profiles of you or sell your information.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where data protection law such as the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, processing in connection with the App relies on the following legal bases: your consent, for the use of advertising technologies that store or access information on your device and for personalised advertising; and legitimate interests, for providing a free, ad-supported game, measuring advertising and preventing fraud. Storage of your game data on your device and in your iCloud account is necessary to provide the App to you.

6. Data retention

Game data remains on your device until you delete the App, and in your iCloud account until you remove it through your device's iCloud settings. We do not retain any copy. Information collected by Google is retained in accordance with Google's policies.

7. International transfers

Google and Apple may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. These companies are responsible for providing appropriate safeguards for such transfers under applicable law.

8. Security

Your game data is protected by the security features of your device and of your Apple Account. Because we do not collect or store personal information, there is no personal information held by us that could be exposed.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no accounts, chat or social features, and its advertising is limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information about you, requests concerning advertising data should be directed to Google, which you can do through Google's privacy controls. You can also:

  • delete your game data by uninstalling the App;
  • remove the iCloud copy by managing iCloud storage in your device's Settings;
  • allow or stop tracking at any time in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking;
  • review or change your advertising consent with "Privacy choices" in the App's Settings, where applicable.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. California residents

We do not sell personal information for money. If you allow tracking, the information Google receives to show you personalised ads may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act. You can opt out of this at any time by turning tracking off in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. With tracking off, the App requests non-personalised ads only. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or services. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes on the App Store.
